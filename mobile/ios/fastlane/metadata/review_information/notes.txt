Outpost is self-hosted, so this app needs a server address before you can sign in.

Use the reference instance at outpost.sonofatech.com. It is prefilled on the sign in screen.

Sign in with the review account supplied in the demo account fields, then open any channel under TEXT CHANNELS to see messaging, or a channel under VOICE CHANNELS to test voice and video. Voice needs microphone permission.

Source for this build is public at github.com/blindrun/outpost-chat
